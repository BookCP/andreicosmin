Selected Work

IT Support Help Center Redesign
DeepL · 2025–2026 · Service Design

The legacy portal forced 1,200+ employees to navigate IT categories before they could describe their problem. I analysed 283 tickets to identify misrouting patterns, then replaced 15+ legacy request types with six intent-based forms.

The design was grounded in behavioural research: Hick's Law to limit choices, progressive disclosure to reduce cognitive load, and decision-fatigue awareness to keep forms under seven questions. Every jargon term was replaced with plain language — "SSO" became "connect a tool with DeepL login."

Escalation & Collaboration Model
DeepL · 2026 · ITSM Architecture

The support team had no formal escalation path between L1, L2, and L3. Escalations happened in Slack DMs and nothing was recorded. I designed a complete operating model with workflow transitions, SLA clock management, and queue routing.

The model included a de-escalation path with mandatory feedback comments, so L1 could learn from specialist work. I also proposed a balanced scorecard replacing ticket-count KPIs with complexity-weighted metrics, escalation rates, and knowledge-article contributions.
